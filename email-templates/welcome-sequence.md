## MailerLite Automation Setup Instructions

### Step 1: Create the Automation Workflow

1. Log in to your MailerLite dashboard
2. Go to **Automations** → **Create automation**
3. Choose **"Start from scratch"**
4. Name it: "Reader's Club Welcome Sequence"

### Step 2: Set the Trigger

1. Click **"Choose a trigger"**
2. Select **"Subscriber joins a group"**
3. Choose your Reader's Club group (ID: 142622019444278286)

### Step 3: Add the Email Sequence

**Email 1: Welcome (Immediate)**
- Click **"+"** → **"Send email"**
- Delay: **0 hours** (immediate)
- Create new email, paste Email 1 HTML template
- Subject: "Welcome to the Reader's Club! 🎉"

**Email 2: Content Highlights (Day 1)**
- Click **"+"** → **"Delay"** → **1 day**
- Click **"+"** → **"Send email"**
- Create new email, paste Email 2 HTML template
- Subject: "Your First Exclusive Bonus Chapter Awaits 📖"

**Email 3: Character Interview (Day 3)**
- Click **"+"** → **"Delay"** → **2 days**
- Click **"+"** → **"Send email"**
- Create new email, paste Email 3 HTML template
- Subject: "Sophia Carter Answers Your Burning Questions 💬"

**Email 4: Behind the Scenes (Day 7)**
- Click **"+"** → **"Delay"** → **4 days**
- Click **"+"** → **"Send email"**
- Create new email, paste Email 4 HTML template
- Subject: "How the Manhattan Money Kings Came to Life ✨"

### Step 4: Activate the Automation

1. Review the workflow
2. Click **"Activate"**
3. New Reader's Club members will automatically receive this sequence!

### Step 5: Monitor Performance

- Go to **Automations** → **Reader's Club Welcome Sequence**
- View open rates, click rates, and subscriber journey
- Adjust timing or content based on performance

## Customization Tips

1. **Personalization**: Use `{{subscriber.name}}` or `{{subscriber.first_name}}` for personal touches
2. **URLs**: Update all links to match your actual domain when published
3. **Timing**: Adjust delays based on your audience's engagement patterns
4. **A/B Testing**: Test different subject lines to improve open rates
5. **Mobile**: All templates are mobile-responsive by default

---

## Success Metrics to Track

- **Open Rate**: Aim for 40-50% (industry standard for welcome emails)
- **Click Rate**: Aim for 15-25% (shows engagement with exclusive content)
- **Unsubscribe Rate**: Should be <1% for welcome sequences
- **Content Access**: Track which exclusive content gets the most clicks
