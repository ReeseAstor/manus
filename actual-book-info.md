# Actual Book Information - Manhattan Money Kings Series

## Book 1: The Billionaire's Executive Assistant
**Characters:**
- Sarah Shah (protagonist) - needs job to pay mother's medical bills
- Michael Hastings (love interest) - CEO of Chen Technologies

**Plot:** Sarah needs the job for her mother's mounting medical bills. Michael needs a competent assistant for his billion-dollar Redwood project. When Sarah uncovers financial sabotage orchestrated by Michael's ruthless competitor, they're forced into a dangerous alliance that crosses every professional boundary.

**Setting:** Chen Technologies, Manhattan

---

## Book 2: The Billionaire's Fake Wife  
**Characters:**
- Elena Kim/Elena Volkov (protagonist) - thief, daughter of Yuri Volkov
- David Kim (love interest) - billionaire CEO

**Plot:** Elena breaks into David Kim's penthouse to steal diamonds/secrets for her violent father. David catches her and offers a deal: marry him for 12 months in exchange for protection from her father and compensation.

**Key Details:**
- Elena is Yuri Volkov's daughter
- David Kim is 35, Harvard MBA, Fortune 500 CEO by 30
- Setting: Upper East Side penthouse, Manhattan
- Enemies-to-lovers, forced marriage, international suspense

---

## Book 3: The Billionaire's Baby Secret
**Characters:**
- Sophia Reed (protagonist) - single mother, works in corporate marketing  
- Dominic Kane (love interest) - CEO, rising mogul
- Bella - their 3-year-old daughter with storm-gray eyes

**Plot:** Three years ago, Sophia and Dominic had a one-night stand. She got pregnant but he never returned her calls (his assistant blocked them). Now he's back and discovers Bella has his storm-gray eyes. Sophia works at Kane Industries.

**Key Details:**
- Bella calls him "Dom-Dom" (nickname Sophia used)
- Setting: Kane Tower, Hudson Yards, Manhattan
- Daycare: Little Stars Daycare in Tribeca
- Sophia's boss: Tara Liu (CMO)

---

## Prequel: The Billionaire's One Night Stand
**Characters:**
- Victoria Cruz (protagonist) - attorney at Stanton & Pierce law firm
- James Chen (love interest) - billionaire tech CEO

**Plot:** Victoria gets passed over for law firm partnership (Andrew Keller gets it instead). She meets James Chen at an upscale Manhattan rooftop bar. They have one night together. Monday she discovers he's her new boss/client.

**Key Details:**
- Victoria drinks $1,500 whiskey on corporate card
- James Chen: Forbes "most feared negotiator in Silicon Valley"
- Setting: Manhattan rooftop bar, corporate law offices
- Enemies-to-lovers, workplace romance

---

## Series Order:
- Prequel: The Billionaire's One Night Stand
- Book 1: The Billionaire's Executive Assistant  
- Book 2: The Billionaire's Fake Wife
- Book 3: The Billionaire's Baby Secret

All set in Manhattan, billionaire romance with high heat, guaranteed HEA.
