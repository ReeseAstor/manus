Good to know

Where should I start the series?

Anywhere, since every book stands alone. Most readers start with Book 1, The Billionaire’s Executive Assistant, or try the prequel novella first.

Do I need to read them in order?

No. Every book is a complete standalone with its own couple and a guaranteed HEA. Reading in order adds background.

How steamy are they?

Explicit, open-door scenes. The books are written for readers 18 and over.
