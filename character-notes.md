# Manhattan Money Kings Series - Character Reference

## Book 1: The Billionaire's Executive Assistant
**Main Characters:**
- **Sarah Shah** - Executive assistant, working to pay mother's medical bills
- **Michael Hastings** - Billionaire CEO of Chen Technologies
- **Relationship:** Boss/assistant, workplace romance, corporate espionage
- **Plot:** Sarah uncovers financial sabotage, forced into dangerous alliance with Michael, forbidden attraction amid corporate intrigue

## Book 2: The Billionaire's Fake Wife
**Main Characters:**
- **Elena Kim** - Professional thief/heist specialist
- **David Kim** - Billionaire, built empire on control and precision
- **Relationship:** Enemies-to-lovers, forced marriage, international suspense
- **Plot:** Elena breaks into David's penthouse to steal diamonds, gets caught, they end up in a fake marriage arrangement

## Book 3: The Billionaire's Baby Secret
**Main Characters:**
- **Sophia Reed** - Single mother, works in corporate marketing
- **Dominic Kane** - Rising mogul/CEO who controls outcomes
- **Relationship:** Second chance romance, secret baby
- **Plot:** One night stand 3 years ago, Sophia has daughter Bella with Dominic's storm-gray eyes, he returns and discovers the truth

## Prequel: The Billionaire's One Night Stand
**Main Characters:**
- **Victoria Cruz** - Attorney denied law firm partnership
- **James Chen** - Billionaire tech CEO, ruthless negotiator
- **Relationship:** One night stand turns into workplace complication
- **Plot:** Victoria and James have savage night together, Monday she discovers he's her new boss
