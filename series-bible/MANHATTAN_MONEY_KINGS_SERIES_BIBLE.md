## Series Overview

The Manhattan Money Kings series follows powerful billionaire CEOs navigating love, ambition, and second chances in the glittering world of New York City's elite. Each standalone novel features a different couple while building a shared universe of interconnected characters and businesses.

### Reading Order

- **Prequel:** The Billionaire's One Night Stand (156 pages)
- **Book 1:** The Billionaire's Executive Assistant (190 pages)
- **Book 2:** The Billionaire's Fake Wife (236 pages)
- **Book 3:** The Billionaire's Baby Secret (327 pages)

**Total Series Length:** 909 pages

## MAIN CHARACTERS

### Book 3: The Billionaire's Baby Secret

#### Dominic Kane
- **Full Name:** Dominic Kane
- **Age:** Early-to-mid 30s (approximately 32-35)
- **Occupation:** CEO, Kane Industries (tech/data company)
- **Physical Description:**
  - Storm-gray eyes
  - Precisely trimmed beard
  - Broader through the shoulders
  - Strong jaw
  - Commanding presence
  - Wears deep charcoal Brioni suits
- **Personality Traits:**
  - Ruthless businessman
  - Measured and deliberate in movements
  - Accustomed to immediate obedience
  - Controls outcomes professionally
  - Has "new, deliberate restraint" after 3-year absence
  - Voice carries subtle rasp
  - Long memory and longer reach in business
- **Background:**
  - Rising mogul in tech industry
  - Has toppled Fortune 500 competitors
  - Charmed senators
  - Commands respect through silence
  - Spent time away (implied international business)
- **Relationships:**
  - Father to Bella (daughter, age 2)
  - Former lover of Sophia Reed (3 years ago)
  - Unknown father status at book start
- **Nickname:** "Dom-Dom" (used by Sophia in intimate moments)
- **Character Arc:** Learning to be a father and partner, transitioning from control to care

#### Sophia Reed
- **Full Name:** Sophia Reed
- **Age:** 28 (25 three years prior)
- **Occupation:** Marketing/Strategy professional at company doing business with Kane Industries
- **Physical Description:**
  - Dark features
  - Wears Chanel lipstick (splurge item, considered "armor")
  - Professional appearance
  - Careful about presentation
- **Personality Traits:**
  - Resilient and independent
  - Protective mother
  - Careful and vigilant about her past
  - Professional and composed under pressure
  - Built stability "the hard way"
  - Strong work ethic (early trains, late invoices)
- **Background:**
  - Single mother for 3 years
  - Had one-night encounter with Dominic 3 years ago
  - Tried to contact Dominic after pregnancy but was stonewalled
  - Built life alone with daughter
  - Lives in Tribeca
- **Relationships:**
  - Mother to Bella
  - Sister to Emma
  - Former lover of Dominic Kane
  - Works under Tara Liu (CMO)
- **Living Situation:** Small Tribeca apartment in converted warehouse with exposed brick
- **Character Arc:** Balancing independence with allowing Dominic into their lives

#### Bella Kane/Reed
- **Full Name:** Bella (last name unclear - likely Reed, possibly Kane after reveal)
- **Age:** 24 months (2 years old) at book start
- **Physical Description:**
  - Storm-gray eyes (identical to Dominic's)
  - Dark curls
  - Small for her age
- **Personality:**
  - Observant
  - Absorbs world like a sponge
  - Devastating honesty (typical toddler)
  - Asks questions about families
- **Relationships:**
  - Daughter of Sophia Reed and Dominic Kane
  - Niece of Emma
  - Cared for at Little Stars Daycare
- **Belongings:** Beloved stuffed animal named "Star" (constant companion since 6 months old)
- **Development:** Starting to ask about "daddies" and notice family structures

## LOCATIONS & SETTINGS

### Primary Locations

#### Kane Tower
- **Location:** Hudson Yards, Manhattan
- **Description:**
  - 43+ floors
  - Glass and steel construction
  - Floor-to-ceiling windows with Hudson River views
  - Executive boardroom on 43rd floor
  - Private elevator for CEO
  - Massive wall screens for presentations
  - Polished mahogany conference tables
  - Arctic air conditioning
  - Marble floors in lobby
  - Abstract sculptures (expensive)
  - Cathedral-like commercial lobby
  - Revolving doors
  - Security desk with visitor badges
  - Executive level: hushed, dimly lit, polished surfaces, muted colors
- **Significance:** Dominic Kane's corporate headquarters, symbol of power

#### Sophia's Tribeca Apartment
- **Location:** Tribeca, Manhattan
- **Building Type:** Converted warehouse
- **Features:**
  - Exposed brick
  - Industrial fixtures
  - 3 flights of stairs (no elevator mentioned)
  - Small but warm
  - East-facing windows (morning sun)
  - Kitchen table
- **Rent:** "Trendy enough to justify the rent, affordable enough for single mother's budget"
- **Contents:**
  - Picture books and building blocks
  - Finger paints and construction paper
  - Couch with toddler blankets
  - Hallway mirror
- **Significance:** Sophia's safe haven, symbol of independence

#### Little Stars Daycare
- **Location:** Tribeca, Manhattan (20 blocks south of Kane Tower)
- **Building Type:** Cheerful brownstone
- **Features:**
  - Hand-painted murals
  - Small playground with wrought-iron fencing
  - Cubbies for tiny coats
  - Walls covered in finger-painted art
  - Quiet room (dimly lit, soft cushions, rocking chair)
- **Staff:** Mrs. Santos, Miss Jennifer
- **Significance:** Bella's daily care, represents Sophia's working mother reality
