# Best Billionaire Romance Series to Binge-Read

*Looking for your next book boyfriend? These billionaire romance series deliver swoon-worthy heroes, emotional depth, and the kind of chemistry that keeps you reading until 3 AM.*

There's something irresistible about billionaire romance. Maybe it's the fantasy of being swept off your feet by someone who could buy you anything but chooses to give you everything that matters instead. Or perhaps it's watching powerful men who control boardrooms become completely undone by love. Whatever draws you to the genre, there are some exceptional series worth adding to your TBR pile.

I've read more billionaire romance than I can count (it's basically research for my day job), and these are the series I genuinely recommend to friends. No filler picks. Every one of these kept me up past midnight.

## What Makes a Great Billionaire Romance Series?

Before diving into recommendations, let's talk about what separates a forgettable billionaire romance from one that stays with you long after you've finished the last page.

**Complex Heroes**: The most compelling billionaire heroes aren't just wealthy. They're layered characters with genuine struggles, past wounds, and room to grow. A man who has everything except the one thing money can't buy creates natural tension and emotional stakes.

**Strong Heroines**: Great billionaire romance features women who challenge their heroes, who have their own goals and dreams, and who don't simply exist to be rescued. The best heroines make their billionaires earn their happily ever after.

**Emotional Depth**: Beyond the glamorous settings and steamy scenes, the series that resonate most deeply explore real themes: trust, vulnerability, family, and what it truly means to build a life with someone.

## The Manhattan Money Kings Series by Reese Astor

If you're searching for a billionaire romance series that checks every box (sizzling chemistry, emotional depth, stunning Manhattan settings, and heroes who will make you swoon), the **Manhattan Money Kings** series deserves the top spot on your reading list.

Set against the glittering backdrop of New York City's elite, this series follows powerful men whose carefully controlled lives are upended by the women who refuse to play by their rules. What sets this series apart is how the fantasy elements of billionaire romance are balanced with genuine emotional authenticity.

### Book 1: The Billionaire's Executive Assistant

The series opens with **Michael Hastings**, Manhattan's most ruthless dealmaker, and **Sarah Shah**, the executive assistant who sees through his carefully constructed walls. This forbidden office romance crackles with tension from the first page. Sarah isn't intimidated by Michael's reputation or his billions. She's there to do a job, not fall for her impossible boss.

*Tropes: Boss and Assistant, Forbidden Romance, Corporate Intrigue*

### Book 2: The Billionaire's Fake Wife

The second installment introduces **David Kim**, a billionaire CEO, in a fake marriage romance that becomes all too real. When **Elena Volkov** breaks into David's penthouse under pressure from her criminal father, David sees an opportunity, and Elena sees a way out.

*Tropes: Fake Marriage, Enemies to Lovers, Forced Proximity*

### Book 3: The Billionaire's Baby Secret

Book 3 brings together **Dominic Kane** and **Sophia Reed** in a secret baby romance that will have you reaching for tissues. Almost four years after one snowbound weekend in Chicago, Sophia has built a life for herself and her daughter, a daughter with unmistakable storm-gray eyes that match the billionaire who just walked back into her life.

*Tropes: Secret Baby, Second Chance, Single Mom Romance*

### Book 4: The Billionaire's Forbidden Nanny

**Camille Torres** takes a job caring for billionaire **Ethan Calloway**'s silent daughter and never expects to become the family they both need. Ethan hasn't smiled since his wife died, his five-year-old hasn't spoken, and seven nannies have quit. The last thing he needs is a nanny with quick wit, warm hands, and a mouth that talks back.

*Tropes: Nanny Romance, Single Dad, Forbidden Romance*

**Start the series free**: The prequel novella, *The Billionaire's One Night Stand*, is available as a free download and introduces readers to the world of Manhattan's elite.

## Kings of Sin Series by Ana Huang

Ana Huang writes billionaire romance with razor-sharp wit and enough heat to melt your Kindle. The Kings of Sin series follows a group of powerful men, each defined by a deadly sin, as they fall for women who challenge everything they thought they wanted.

[King of Wrath](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B36FKPS8?tag=reeseastor-20) kicks off the series with an arranged marriage between a ruthless CEO and a woman who refuses to be controlled. The tension between Dante and Vivian is exquisite. They're forced together by circumstance but kept apart by pride, and watching them crack each other's defenses is deeply satisfying.

What I love about this series is that Huang doesn't shy away from the darker edges of wealth and power. These aren't sanitized billionaires. They're complicated, sometimes morally gray, and always compelling.

## Twisted Series by Ana Huang

If you prefer your billionaire romance with a darker edge, Huang's [Twisted Love](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08Y6DCS1Y?tag=reeseastor-20) delivers. Alex Volkov is cold, calculating, and hiding secrets that could destroy everything. When he becomes the reluctant guardian of his best friend's sister, the tension is almost unbearable.

The entire Twisted series follows interconnected characters in a world of wealth and danger. Each book works as a standalone, but reading them in order rewards you with callbacks and character development that builds across the series.

## The Deal by Elle Kennedy

[The Deal](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00TG1CZFC?tag=reeseastor-20) isn't strictly billionaire romance (it's college romance), but I'm including it because it's one of the most-recommended books in the genre for good reason. If you're looking for something lighter between heavier reads, this delivers banter, chemistry, and a hero who earns his HEA in the most satisfying way.

Elle Kennedy writes dialogue that crackles, and the Off-Campus series has become a gateway drug for countless romance readers. Start here if you want something fun before diving back into the high-stakes world of billionaires.

## How to Choose Your Next Billionaire Romance Series

With so many options available, finding the right series for your reading mood can feel overwhelming. Consider these factors:

**Heat Level**: Billionaire romance spans from sweet to scorching. The Manhattan Money Kings series and Kings of Sin both offer steamy scenes balanced with emotional development, perfect for readers who want both.

**Tropes**: Are you in the mood for enemies to lovers? Fake relationship? Secret baby? Most series lean into specific tropes, so identifying what you're craving helps narrow your options.

**Setting**: Some readers love the glamour of New York City, while others prefer small-town settings or international backdrops. Manhattan Money Kings is perfect for NYC lovers.

**Series Length**: Consider whether you want a trilogy you can finish in a weekend or a longer series to savor over time.
