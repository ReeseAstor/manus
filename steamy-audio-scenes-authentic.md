## Scene 1: Elena & David - The Diamond Thief's Surrender

**From: The Billionaire's Fake Wife (Book 2)**  
**Characters:** Elena Kim (thief) & David Kim (billionaire)

The penthouse security system was supposed to be impenetrable. Elena had proven otherwise, slipping through David Kim's defenses like smoke through glass. But now, caught red-handed with his diamonds glittering in her palm, she faced a far more dangerous threat than any alarm system.

David stood between her and the exit, his tailored suit immaculate despite the late hour, his eyes dark with something that wasn't quite anger. "You're either incredibly brave or incredibly foolish, Ms. Kim," he said, his voice low and controlled. "Breaking into my home. Stealing from me."

Elena's pulse raced, but she kept her chin high. "I prefer 'resourceful.'" She'd researched him thoroughly—billionaire, control freak, built his empire on precision and never letting anyone close. She hadn't expected him to be this... magnetic.

"Resourceful." He moved closer, each step deliberate. "Is that what you call it when you bypass a million-dollar security system?"

"I call it Tuesday." She should run. Every instinct screamed at her to move, but his proximity pinned her in place more effectively than any restraints.

"You have skills," David murmured, now close enough that she could smell his cologne—expensive, masculine, intoxicating. "Skills I could use. But first, I need to know if I can trust you."

"You can't." The honesty slipped out before she could stop it. "I'm a thief. Trust isn't in my vocabulary."

His lips curved into something predatory. "Then we'll have to teach you." His hand reached out, not to grab her, but to trace the line of her jaw with surprising gentleness. "Starting now."

Elena's breath caught. This wasn't how heists were supposed to end. "What are you proposing?"

"A deal." His thumb brushed her lower lip. "You work for me. Use those skills for my purposes instead of against me. In return, I don't call the police."

"And if I refuse?"

"Then I'll have to find other ways to keep you close." The promise in his voice sent heat flooding through her. "Because now that I've caught you, Elena Kim, I'm not letting you disappear into the night."

She should negotiate. Should demand terms, set boundaries. Instead, she found herself leaning into his touch. "You're dangerous."

"So are you." His mouth hovered inches from hers. "That's what makes this interesting."

When he finally kissed her, it wasn't gentle—it was claiming, demanding, a collision of two people who'd spent their lives trusting no one. Elena's hands fisted in his shirt, the diamonds forgotten as she surrendered to something far more valuable and far more terrifying than any heist.

This was the beginning of her most dangerous job yet: stealing the heart of a man who'd sworn never to let anyone in.

## Scene 2: Sarah & Michael - After Hours at Chen Technologies

**From: The Billionaire's Executive Assistant (Book 1)**  
**Characters:** Sarah Shah (assistant) & Michael Hastings (CEO)

The office was empty, the Manhattan skyline glittering through floor-to-ceiling windows as Sarah Shah reviewed the Redwood project files for the third time. She'd been Michael Hastings' executive assistant for three months, and every day tested her resolve to keep things professional.

"You're still here." His voice from the doorway made her jump. Michael stood there in shirtsleeves, his tie loosened, looking more human than the controlled CEO who commanded boardrooms.

"The presentation needs to be perfect," Sarah said, trying to ignore how her pulse quickened. She needed this job—her mother's medical bills depended on it. She couldn't afford to be distracted by her impossibly attractive boss.

Michael crossed to her desk, his presence filling the space. "It's already perfect. You've checked it twice."

"Three times, actually." She met his gaze, saw something there that made her breath catch—heat, barely restrained, simmering beneath his professional facade.

"Sarah." The way he said her name, low and rough, sent shivers down her spine. "We need to talk about this."

"About what?" She knew exactly what. The tension that crackled between them every time they were alone. The accidental touches that lingered too long. The way his eyes followed her across the office.

"About the fact that I can't stop thinking about you." He braced his hands on her desk, leaning close. "About how every time you challenge me in meetings, I want to kiss that smart mouth of yours. About how unprofessional and completely necessary this has become."

Sarah's heart hammered. "You're my boss. This is—"

"Inappropriate? Reckless?" His hand cupped her face. "I know. But I've built my career on calculated risks, and you, Sarah Shah, are the best risk I've ever considered taking."

"Michael—" His name was a plea, a warning, a surrender all at once.

"Tell me to stop," he murmured, his lips a breath away from hers. "Tell me you don't feel this too, and I'll walk away. We'll pretend this never happened."

But Sarah couldn't lie. Not when every cell in her body screamed for his touch. Not when she'd spent three months fighting an attraction that only grew stronger. "I can't tell you that."

"Good." His kiss was everything she'd imagined and more—demanding yet tender, controlled yet desperate. His hands slid into her hair as he pulled her up from her chair, pressing her against the desk.

"This is crazy," Sarah gasped between kisses. "If anyone finds out—"

"Let them." Michael's mouth found the sensitive spot below her ear. "I don't care about office politics. I care about you."

The Redwood project files scattered as he lifted her onto the desk, his body fitting perfectly between her thighs. Outside, Manhattan pulsed with life, but in this moment, their world narrowed to just the two of them—boss and assistant, crossing every professional line and discovering that some risks were worth taking.

## Scene 3: Dominic & Sophia - The Reunion

**From: The Billionaire's Baby Secret (Book 3)**  
**Characters:** Sophia Reed (single mother) & Dominic Kane (CEO)

Three years. That's how long Sophia had kept her secret. Three years since that one perfect night with Dominic Kane had changed everything. Three years since she'd walked away, pregnant and alone, because the rising mogul had made it clear he didn't do relationships or complications.

Now he stood in her office doorway, those storm-gray eyes—the same shade as their daughter Bella's—fixed on her with an intensity that stole her breath.

"Sophia Reed." He said her name like a discovery, like a man who'd just found something he'd been searching for. "I didn't expect to see you here."

"Dominic." She forced her voice to remain steady, professional. He couldn't know. Couldn't find out about Bella. "I work here. Marketing department."

He moved into her office, commanding the space effortlessly. He looked different—broader, more dangerous, more controlled. Success had honed him into something even more formidable. "I remember you being more... forthcoming. That night in Chicago—"

"Was a mistake." The lie tasted bitter. That night had given her Bella, and she'd never regret her daughter.

"A mistake." His jaw tightened. "Is that what we're calling it?"

"What else would you call a one-night stand with a stranger?" She stood, needing the desk between them as a barrier. "You made it clear you weren't interested in anything more."

"I called you." His voice dropped, dangerous and low. "For weeks. You disappeared."

Sophia's heart stuttered. "I—what?"

"My assistant said you never returned my calls." Dominic closed the distance between them, his presence overwhelming. "I thought you'd moved on. Found someone else. Never knew you were carrying my—"

He stopped, his gaze dropping to the photo frame on her desk. Bella, age three, grinning at the camera with her father's storm-gray eyes.

The silence stretched, terrible and absolute.

"Dominic—" Sophia started, but he was already moving, picking up the frame with shaking hands.

"How old?" His voice was barely a whisper.

"Three." There was no point lying now.

He looked at her, and the raw emotion in his eyes nearly broke her. "You kept this from me. Kept her from me."

"You didn't want complications," Sophia said, tears threatening. "You made that clear. I wasn't going to trap you—"

"Trap me?" He set down the frame carefully, then pulled her against him, his hands framing her face. "Sophia, I spent months trying to find you. I thought I'd lost you. And all this time, I had a daughter?"

"I'm sorry," she whispered. "I thought—"

"No more thinking." His kiss was fierce, claiming, three years of longing compressed into one desperate moment. "No more running. You're mine, Sophia Reed. You and our daughter. And this time, I'm not letting you go."

As his mouth moved over hers, as his hands relearned the curves he'd memorized three years ago, Sophia finally let herself believe that maybe, just maybe, the ruthless CEO had learned that some things were worth more than control. Some things—like love, like family—were worth surrendering everything for.
