## Official Blurb
She's the nanny. He's her boss. His mother wants her gone.

Camille Torres doesn't belong in a Park Avenue penthouse. She belongs in the Bronx, finishing her teaching degree and keeping her family afloat. But when a billionaire offers her a salary that could change everything, she takes the job—and walks straight into trouble.

Ethan Calloway hasn't smiled since his wife died. His five-year-old daughter hasn't spoken. Seven nannies have quit. He doesn't need another complication—especially not one with quick wit, warm hands, and a mouth that talks back.

But Lily starts drawing again. Then laughing. Then reaching for Camille like she's safety.

And Ethan? He's reaching too.

One kiss in the kitchen. One night that changes everything. One secret that his mother will use to destroy them both.

Gloria Calloway doesn't lose. She has money, connections, and a custody lawyer on speed dial. Camille has a thrift-store blazer and the Bronx.

But she also has something Gloria will never understand:
A little girl who finally calls her Mama.

### Camille Torres (Female Protagonist)
- **Age**: 27

- **Occupation**: Teaching assistant at Hunter College, finishing her teaching degree (3 semesters left)

- **Physical Description**: Curly hair, warm hands
- **Character Arc**: Bronx girl who becomes nanny to a billionaire's daughter, eventually becomes Lily's "Mama" and Ethan's wife

### Ethan Calloway (Male Protagonist)
- **Age**: Not specified (appears to be mid-30s)

## Key Plot Points

1. **Meeting**: Camille spills coffee on Ethan in the elevator before her nanny interview
2. **The Job**: Ethan offers $300,000/year salary after Lily connects with Camille through drawing
3. **Connection**: Lily draws three stick figures (Daddy, Mille, Lily) with a heart
4. **Gloria's Attack**: Gloria stages a photo ambush in Central Park with a photographer
5. **Romance**: Ethan and Camille fall in love despite the forbidden nature
6. **Conflict**: Gloria threatens custody and uses Camille's background against them
7. **Resolution**: Ethan protects Camille and Lily; they get married
8. **Epilogue**: One year later, Camille is pregnant, Lily calls her "Mama," Gloria is gone (court order)
