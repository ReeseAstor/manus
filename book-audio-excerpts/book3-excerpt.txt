Three years ago, Sophia Reed had one unforgettable night with Dominic Kane. Now he's back—and discovers the daughter with his storm-gray eyes that she's been raising alone.

When Dominic learns the truth, he's determined to claim both Sophia and their child. But can Sophia trust the man who disappeared without a trace?

**Scene: The Reunion**

"Dom-Dom!" Bella's excited squeal cuts through the office noise.

My blood runs cold. I spin around to see my three-year-old daughter racing toward the tall, dark-haired man in the impeccably tailored suit—Dominic Kane, my new boss. The man I haven't seen in three years. The man who doesn't know he's a father.

His storm-gray eyes—the same eyes that stare back at me from Bella's face every morning—widen in shock as he takes in the little girl running toward him.

"Sophia?" His voice is rough, disbelieving. His gaze drops to Bella, then snaps back to me. I watch the realization dawn across his face, see the exact moment he connects the dots.

"We need to talk," I manage, my voice barely steady.

"Yes," he says, his jaw tight. "We do." He crouches down to Bella's level, and my heart clenches as I see him truly look at her for the first time. "Hello, sweetheart. What's your name?"

"Bella!" she announces proudly. "I'm three!"

Dominic's hand trembles slightly as he reaches out to touch her dark curls—his curls. When he looks up at me again, his eyes are blazing with a mixture of fury and something that looks dangerously like hope.
