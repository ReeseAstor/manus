**Book 0.5: The Billionaire's One Night Stand - Audio Sample Excerpt**

Victoria Cruz was just trying to close the biggest deal of her career. James Chen was supposed to be her business partner, nothing more.

One night of passion changes everything—and sets the stage for the Manhattan Money Kings series.

**Scene: The Night That Started It All**

"This is a mistake," I whisper, even as James backs me against the floor-to-ceiling windows of his penthouse suite.

"Probably," he agrees, his voice low and rough. His hands frame my face, thumbs brushing my cheekbones. "But I've wanted you since the moment you walked into that boardroom and challenged every assumption I had about this partnership."

The city glitters forty stories below us, but all I can see is the heat in his dark eyes.

"We have a deal to close tomorrow," I remind him, my last attempt at reason. "This could complicate everything."

"Victoria." My name on his lips is half-plea, half-command. "For once, stop thinking about the deal. Stop thinking about tomorrow. Just... feel this."

His mouth hovers inches from mine, giving me one last chance to walk away. But I've been fighting this pull between us for weeks, and I'm done resisting.

"One night," I breathe. "Just tonight."

"Just tonight," he echoes—a promise we both know is a lie.

Because one night with James Chen will never be enough.
