**Book 2: The Billionaire's Fake Wife - Audio Sample Excerpt**

Elena Kim never wanted to be a thief. But when David Kim catches her red-handed stealing a priceless diamond, he makes her an offer she can't refuse: become his fake wife to secure his inheritance, or go to prison.

What starts as a business arrangement ignites into something far more dangerous when their fake marriage feels too real.

**Scene: Caught in the Act**

The diamond glitters in my palm, cold and perfect. Victory surges through me—until I hear his voice.

"Put it down."

I freeze. My heart hammers against my ribs as I turn to face David Kim, the billionaire heir whose family owns this entire collection. His dark eyes pin me in place, calculating and dangerous.

"I can explain—"

"Save it." He steps closer, his presence overwhelming in the dim gallery. "You have two choices, Elena. Prison... or you can help me."

My breath catches. "Help you?"

A slow, predatory smile curves his lips. "Marry me. Play the perfect wife for six months. In return, I'll forget I ever saw you here tonight."

The diamond burns in my hand. I should run. Instead, I find myself asking, "What's the catch?"

"The catch," he murmurs, his gaze dropping to my lips, "is that you'll have to convince everyone—including my family—that you're madly in love with me."
