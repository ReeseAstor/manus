## PROLOGUE: LILY

*[Soft ambient sound: distant city traffic, a child's laughter fading]*

---

**NARRATOR (LILY'S INTERNAL VOICE):**

I wasn't supposed to fall in love with him.

*[Beat]*

That was the first rule. The only rule that mattered. And I'd broken it so completely, so irrevocably, that standing here now—in the marble foyer of his penthouse with my resignation letter clutched in my trembling hands—felt like watching my own heart shatter in slow motion.

*[Sound: Elevator ding, distant]*

Three months ago, I walked into Ethan Cole's world as a temporary solution to his permanent problem. His daughter, Emma, needed stability. Routine. Someone who wouldn't leave.

*[Pause]*

I was supposed to be that someone. The professional. The nanny who kept boundaries as crisp as hospital corners and emotions as contained as the carefully labeled bins in Emma's playroom.

*[Sound: A child's giggle, closer now]*

But Emma... God, Emma. She crawled into my heart the first day, with her serious gray eyes—*his* eyes—and her quiet way of watching everything, trusting nothing. She'd been abandoned by her mother at eighteen months old. Left in a penthouse that cost more than most people's lifetime earnings, with a father who didn't know how to hold her without looking terrified.

*[Beat]*

And Ethan...

*[Longer pause, breath]*

Ethan was never supposed to be anything more than my employer. The cold, controlled billionaire who scheduled his daughter's bedtime stories like board meetings and hadn't smiled—truly smiled—in the two years since his wife walked out.

*[Sound: Footsteps on marble, approaching]*

But I saw him. The real him. The man who checked on Emma four times a night when she had a fever. Who learned to braid hair from YouTube tutorials at 2 AM because she wanted to look like the other girls at preschool. Who stood in her doorway every night after she fell asleep, watching her breathe like he couldn't quite believe she was real.

*[Voice softening]*

I saw the cracks in his armor. And instead of keeping my distance, I walked right through them.

*[Sound: Door opening]*

Now his ex-wife is back. Threatening custody. Threatening everything. And the tabloids have photos of us—of *me*—leaving his building at midnight, looking exactly like what we are.

*[Beat]*

What we were.

*[Sound: A man's voice, distant, calling "Lily?"]*

I have to leave. For Emma's sake. For the custody case. For every logical, rational reason that made sense when I wrote this letter at 3 AM with tears streaming down my face.

*[Pause]*

But as I hear his footsteps approaching—as I prepare to hand him this paper that will end everything—I realize the truth I've been running from since the first time he looked at me like I was the answer to a question he'd forgotten how to ask.

*[Voice breaking slightly]*

I'm not leaving because I don't love him.

I'm leaving because I do.

*[Sound: Footsteps stop. Silence.]*

And loving Ethan Cole might be the most dangerous thing I've ever done.

---

*[Music swells softly, then fades]*

**NARRATOR (TRANSITION):**

*The Billionaire's Forbidden Nanny* — Manhattan Money Kings Book 4. 

Pre-order now for just ninety-nine cents. Official release: February 3rd, 2026.

*[Beat]*

Some rules are made to be broken.
