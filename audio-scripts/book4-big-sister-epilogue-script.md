## BIG SISTER: A BONUS EPILOGUE

### Lily's POV — Six Months Later

*[Sound: Soft morning ambiance—birds, distant city sounds, coffee brewing]*

---

**NARRATOR (LILY):**

I used to think happiness was something you had to earn. Something you worked toward, sacrificed for, and maybe—if you were lucky—caught glimpses of between the hard parts of life.

*[Sound: Coffee cup being set down]*

I was wrong.

Happiness, I've learned, is waking up at 6 AM to tiny feet padding across hardwood floors. It's the weight of a three-year-old climbing into bed between you and the man you love, announcing that the sun is awake so *everyone* should be awake.

*[Sound: Child's giggle, muffled]*

"Lily." Emma's voice is a stage whisper that could wake the entire building. "Lily, are you sleeping?"

I keep my eyes closed, fighting a smile. "Yes. Very deeply asleep."

*[Beat]*

"But your eyes are moving."

*[Soft laugh]*

I open one eye to find her face inches from mine, those serious gray eyes—*Ethan's* eyes—studying me with the intensity of a tiny scientist. Her dark hair is a wild halo around her head, and she's wearing the princess pajamas she insisted on even though it's July and approximately nine hundred degrees outside.

"Caught me." I reach out to smooth her hair. "Good morning, sweet girl."

"Good morning." She snuggles closer, her small body fitting perfectly in the space between us. "Daddy's still sleeping. He snores."

*[Sound: Male grumble from nearby]*

"I do not snore." Ethan's voice is rough with sleep, muffled by his pillow. "I breathe... assertively."

*[Lily laughing]*

Emma giggles—that full-body laugh that still makes my heart squeeze every single time—and launches herself at her father. He catches her with the ease of practice, pulling her onto his chest and tickling her until she shrieks.

*[Sound: Playful struggle, laughter]*

I watch them, these two people who have become my whole world, and feel that familiar swell of emotion that still catches me off guard. Six months ago, I was ready to walk away. To sacrifice my own happiness for what I thought was best for them.

*[Pause]*

Thank God Ethan Cole doesn't take no for an answer.

---

*[Sound: Scene transition—kitchen ambiance, breakfast sounds]*

---

**NARRATOR (LILY):**

"So." Ethan slides a plate of pancakes in front of Emma, then turns to me with that look. The one that says he's about to bring up something I've been avoiding. "Have you thought any more about the wedding?"

*[Sound: Fork clattering]*

I take a very deliberate sip of coffee. "I've thought about it."

"And?"

"And I think Emma needs more syrup."

*[Beat]*

"Nice deflection." He leans against the counter, arms crossed, looking unfairly attractive for 7 AM in a t-shirt and sweatpants. "You've been 'thinking about it' for three weeks. The venue needs an answer."

*[Sigh]*

Here's the thing about marrying a billionaire: everyone has opinions. His family wants a society wedding at The Plaza. My mother wants a traditional Chinese ceremony followed by a banquet for three hundred of her closest friends. The tabloids have been speculating about everything from a destination wedding in Bali to a secret ceremony that already happened.

*[Pause]*

And all I want... is him. And Emma. And maybe a beach somewhere quiet, where no one's watching.

"I don't need a big wedding, Ethan."

"I know." He moves closer, tucking a strand of hair behind my ear. "But you deserve one. You deserve everything."

*[Soft breath]*

"I have everything." I catch his hand, press a kiss to his palm. "I have you. I have Emma. I have a family. That's more than I ever thought I'd have."

*[Sound: Small voice interrupting]*

"Can I be in the wedding?"

We both turn to Emma, who's watching us with syrup on her chin and hope in her eyes.

"Of course, baby." I reach over to wipe her face. "You're the most important part."

"Can I wear a princess dress?"

"The princess-est dress we can find."

*[Beat]*

"Can I have a baby brother?"

*[Silence]*

Ethan chokes on his coffee. I feel my face flush approximately seventeen shades of red. Emma just blinks at us, completely unaware that she's just detonated a conversational bomb at the breakfast table.

"A... brother?" Ethan manages.

"Or a sister." Emma shrugs, returning to her pancakes. "Sophia at school has a baby brother. He cries a lot but she gets to be the big sister. I want to be a big sister."

*[Long pause]*

I look at Ethan. He looks at me. And in that moment, something passes between us—a question, an answer, a future we haven't discussed but suddenly feels inevitable.

*[Softly]*

"We should probably talk about that," I whisper.

"Yeah." His voice is rough. "We probably should."

---

*[Sound: Scene transition—evening, soft music, intimate atmosphere]*

---

**NARRATOR (LILY):**

Later that night, after Emma's asleep and the penthouse is quiet, I find Ethan on the terrace. The city glitters below us, a million lights that used to feel cold and distant but now feel like home.

*[Sound: Terrace door opening, city ambiance]*

"Hey." I wrap my arms around him from behind, pressing my cheek to his back. "You've been quiet."

"Thinking."

"Dangerous."

*[Soft laugh]*

He turns in my arms, pulling me close. The way he looks at me still takes my breath away—like I'm something precious, something he can't quite believe is real.

"Do you want that?" His voice is careful. "A baby?"

*[Pause]*

"Do you?"

"I asked first."

*[Beat]*

I think about it. Really think about it. A year ago, I was a nanny with student debt and a broken engagement and no idea what my future held. Now I'm standing in a penthouse with a man who loves me, a little girl who calls me Mommy, and the possibility of... more.

*[Emotional breath]*

"I want whatever life looks like with you." I meet his eyes. "If that includes a baby—our baby—then yes. I want that. But only if you do. Only if you're ready."

*[Long pause]*

"I wasn't ready for Emma." His voice is quiet, honest. "I wasn't ready for you. I wasn't ready for any of this. But it happened anyway, and it's the best thing that ever happened to me."

*[Beat]*

"So yes, Lily. I want a baby with you. I want a wedding—small, on a beach, just us and Emma and the people who matter. I want to wake up next to you every morning for the rest of my life and fall asleep next to you every night. I want *everything* with you."

*[Sound: Soft kiss]*

"Then let's have everything."

---

*[Sound: Scene transition—soft, dreamy]*

---

**NARRATOR (LILY):**

Three months later, I'm standing on a beach in the Maldives with sand between my toes and Ethan's hands in mine. Emma is our flower girl, scattering petals with the seriousness of a surgeon and the enthusiasm of a tornado. Our families are here—small, intimate, exactly what we wanted.

*[Sound: Ocean waves, soft music]*

And I'm pregnant.

We haven't told anyone yet. It's our secret—mine and Ethan's and the tiny life growing inside me that we discovered two weeks ago. But as I say my vows, as I promise to love this man for the rest of my life, I press my hand to my stomach and feel the future stretching out before us.

*[Voice emotional]*

I wasn't supposed to fall in love with him. That was the rule.

*[Beat]*

But some rules...

*[Pause, smile in voice]*

Some rules are made to be broken.

---

*[Music swells—romantic, triumphant, then softens]*

---

**NARRATOR (CLOSING):**

Thank you for reading *The Billionaire's Forbidden Nanny* and for being part of the Manhattan Money Kings family.

This bonus epilogue is exclusively for newsletter subscribers and MVP members. Please don't share or redistribute—it's my gift to you for being here.

*[Beat]*

If you haven't read the other books in the series, you can find them all at reeseastor.com/books. And if you loved Ethan and Lily's story, please consider leaving a review. It means the world to indie authors like me.

*[Warm tone]*

Until next time... happy reading.

*[Sound: Soft kiss, ocean waves fading]*

---

**END OF BONUS EPILOGUE**
