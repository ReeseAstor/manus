## Production Recommendations

### Narrator Selection

For consistency with the Manhattan Money Kings brand, consider:
- **Female narrator** with warm, sophisticated tone
- **Dual POV capability** for Ethan's sections
- **Experience with romance** genre conventions
- **American accent** (New York inflection preferred)

### Sound Design Elements

| Element | Usage | Source Suggestion |
|---------|-------|-------------------|
| City ambiance | Background texture | Artlist, Epidemic Sound |
| Elevator sounds | Scene transitions | Sound effects library |
| Ocean waves | Epilogue beach scenes | Nature recordings |
| Soft piano | Emotional moments | Royalty-free music |
| Child laughter | Emma scenes | Professional recording |

### Technical Specifications

- **Format:** MP3 (320kbps) + M4A for Apple devices
- **Sample Rate:** 44.1kHz
- **Bit Depth:** 16-bit
- **Loudness:** -16 LUFS (ACX standard)
- **Noise Floor:** -60dB or lower

   - Series: Manhattan Money Kings
   - Volume: 4 (Prologue)

   - Format: MP3
   - Enable "Audio Book" delivery option

3. **Landing Page**
